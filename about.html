About

Hi, my name is Belle and I am currently a senior at UW-Milwaukee. I made a wireframe in a past class for an imaginary platform about rating video games, and I decided to expand on that idea by adding the aspect of connecting with others. Game Forward draws inspiration from Letterboxd and IGN’s rating functions and the way users can share opinions with a community of like minded media consumers.
